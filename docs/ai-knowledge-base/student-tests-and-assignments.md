# Tests and assignments (Student)

## Take a test

1. Click **My Tests**: "Multiple-choice tests your teachers have published for your class".
2. Click **Start** on the test.
   - **Not open yet** means it opens later.
   - **Closed** means it can no longer be taken.
3. Answer each question. For "Select all that apply" questions, pick every correct option. Your answers save as you go ("Saving...").
4. Click **Submit test**.
   - If the test has a timer and **time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically**: "Time is up. Your answers have been submitted."
5. Your **Score** and **Your answers** are shown after submitting. Results are calculated automatically.

You can take each test once. Only your teacher can allow a retake.

You see a test only if your teacher assigned it to you, and only for a course you are enrolled in.

## Submit an assignment

1. Click **Assignments**. This page is **My Assignments**, and its lists are **To do** and **Submitted and past**.
2. Open the assignment. Read the questions, the **Total marks** and the **Due** date.
3. Under **Your submission**, choose your answers as a **PDF** and click **Submit assignment**.
4. You will see "Assignment submitted successfully." The status reads "Submitted · awaiting evaluation". **Download** shows the file you sent.
5. When your teacher publishes the grade, **Final result** shows your **Marks** and **Teacher feedback**. You also get a notification.
