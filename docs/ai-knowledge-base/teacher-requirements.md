# Request classroom items (Teacher)

Teachers ask the store for items needed in class.

## Steps

1. In the Mentor sidebar, click **Requirements**.
2. Click **New Requirement**. The form is titled **Create Requirement Request**: "Select items and quantities needed for your classroom session".
3. Fill in:
   - the **Classroom Name** (for example "Grade 10 - Section A");
   - the **Priority**: **Low Priority**, **Medium Priority** or **High Priority**;
   - the items under **Select Items**, with their **Quantities**.
4. Submit the request.

Your requests are listed on the same page with their status. The storekeeper, accountant or admin reviews them.

## Notes

- If the list is empty: "No requirements found. Submit your first requirement request using the button above."
- How the status of a request changes, and what each status means, was not verified. Ask your storekeeper about a specific request.
