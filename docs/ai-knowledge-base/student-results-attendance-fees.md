# Results, attendance and fees (Student)

**In short:** students see their own marks under **Results**, their attendance under **Attendance**, and their fees under **Fees & Payments**.

## Results (your marks)

Click **Results** (**My Results**) to see **your own marks**. Other students' marks are never shown to you. Each term shows:

- the **Classroom**;
- the **Subject-wise Marks**: **Subject**, **Marks**, **Total**, **Percentage**;
- the **Overall Percentage** and the **Status**.

"No results yet" means your teacher has not entered them yet.

## Attendance

Click **Attendance** (**My Attendance**). It shows:

- **Total Classes**, **Present**, **Absent** and **Attendance %**;
- each day with its **Date**, **Classroom** and **Status**.

Attendance is marked by your teacher. Live-class attendance is shown separately, under **Your Lectures**.

## Fees and payments

Click **Fees & Payments**. The page shows:

- your **Total Curriculum Fee**, **Paid to Date** and **Remaining Balance**;
- the **Due Date** and the fee breakdown;
- **Payment History & Official Tax Invoices**. Download a receipt with **PDF Invoice**.

**Online payment is currently switched off.** If you try to pay, the page says "Online payment is not available yet. Please pay at the school office."

Pay at the school office instead. The accountant records the payment, and it then appears in your payment history.

You can see only your own fee records.
