# Descriptive assignments (Teacher)

In a descriptive assignment, students answer written questions and upload a **PDF**.

## Create and publish

1. Go to **AI Assessments**, then **Descriptive Assignments**. The page is titled **Descriptive Assignments Studio**.
2. Click **New Assignment**, or **Create Assignment** if there are none yet.
3. Under **Assignment Details**, enter:
   - **Title**;
   - **Classroom** (from **Select a class**);
   - **Total Marks**;
   - optional: **Due Date**;
   - **Instructions for Students**.
4. Under **Questions & Prompts**, write each question with **Marks for this question**. Use **Add Question**, and reorder with the up and down arrows.
5. Click **Save Draft**, then **Publish Assignment**.

Students of the class get a notification. A published assignment is **Locked** for editing. **Close Assignment** stops new submissions.

## Grade submissions

1. Open the assignment and click **View Submissions**. This is the **Submissions Review** page.
2. For each student you can:
   - **Download PDF** to read the work;
   - **View AI** to see the **AI Suggested Evaluation**. This is private and **hidden from the student**.
3. Click **Grade**:
   - accept the AI proposal with **Accept Suggestions & Finalize**; or
   - use **Edit Manually** to enter the marks and feedback yourself.
4. Click **Save & Publish Final Marks**. This makes the marks and your feedback visible to the student.

If the page says "Descriptive assignments are not available yet (a database update is pending).", the feature has not been switched on for this installation. Contact support.
